## <div align="center">About Me</div>

I'm a software engineer who enjoys building software, understanding how systems work, and turning ideas into useful products.

I like working across different areas of engineering, from backend development and system design to infrastructure and AI powered applications. I enjoy exploring how things work beneath the surface, how different components interact, and how to make systems reliable and maintainable.

Beyond writing code, I'm interested in the problems a product solves, the people who use it, and the decisions that help it succeed. I enjoy exploring product development, customer behavior, business development, and go to market strategy alongside engineering.

My long term goal is to build products of my own and understand the entire process, from identifying a problem and building a solution to finding customers and growing a sustainable business.
